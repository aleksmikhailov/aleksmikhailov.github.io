Taktello — Metronome & Tempo

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Your privacy matters. Taktello is designed to work without collecting or sending your personal information to us.

Information the app collects

Taktello does not collect or transmit personal information. The app does not use analytics, advertising, user accounts, cloud syncing, or crash-reporting services. Metronome use is handled on your device.

How your information is used

Because Taktello does not collect personal information, we do not use or share personal information from the app.

Support requests

If you contact us using the support form, the form asks for your name, email address, topic, and message. Submissions are sent to Formspree to deliver your request. The information you provide there is handled by Formspree under its Privacy Policy. This support process is separate from the Taktello app.

Children’s privacy

Taktello does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s features or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Questions about this policy? Please contact us through the support form.
